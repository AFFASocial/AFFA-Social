## 🌐 About

**AFFASocial** ([affasocial.com](https://www.affasocial.com)) is a long-standing online community built around one core belief: the internet should be a safe, enjoyable, and honest space for everyone. Since 1989, we've been committed to fostering genuine connections without the noise of ads, misinformation, or harmful content.

We don't send advertisements and we'll never ask you to use a temporary email — your experience here is clean by design.

---

## 🛡️ Community Standards

We take our community seriously. Here's what we stand for:

- 🚫 **No political or fake news content** — posts of this nature are removed from all server-wide features
- 🚫 **No adult or explicit content** of any kind, including escort services, dating platforms, or matchmaking sites
- 🚫 **No illegal activity** — zero tolerance, no exceptions
- ✅ **One account per user** — multiple accounts may be deleted without notice
- ✅ **Mutual respect** — treat every member the way you'd want to be treated

> AFFASocial does not host any copyrighted or illegal content. All materials on the platform are legal and sourced from unaffiliated third-party sites such as YouTube.

We reserve the right to refuse, revoke, or delete accounts that violate our terms or community standards.
